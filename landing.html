    How it works

    Four steps from course list to routine.

    1. Choose your courses

      Only courses offered this trimester are listed, and your selection has to fit the credit limit your CGPA allows.

    2. Confirm prerequisites

      Each course shows what it requires. If something is unconfirmed, you go back to selection instead of getting a routine that can't be registered.

    3. Say what you prefer

      Choose a preferred faculty, class time and class days per course, then rank Faculty, Time and Day. Options come from real section data only.

    4. Read your routine

      The solver returns valid routines with class days, times, section and exam slot for every course.

    What "valid" means

    Every routine passes four checks.

    Credit limit

    The credits you request can't exceed the maximum your CGPA permits, and selected courses must add up to the request.

    Prerequisites

    A course such as AI needs DSA completed first. Missing ones are named so you know what to fix.

    Class clashes

    Two sections that share a day and overlap in time can't both be chosen.

    Exam clashes

    Final exam slots are checked the same way, so no two exams land on top of each other.

    Under the hood

    A constraint satisfaction problem.

    Each course is a variable, and its sections are the values it can take. The solver assigns one course at a time, and the moment a choice breaks a constraint it backtracks and tries the next section. That is the trace above.

    Because it works from the same CSV files the app loads, changing a section's time in data/sections.csv changes the routines you get, with no code edits.
